What I Create

Laser Engraving

Turn an idea, image, name, or special message into something made to last. At Crafted Timber Studio, laser engraving combines precision with craftsmanship to create personalized pieces with character and meaning.

From custom gifts and keepsakes to signs, décor, and one-of-a-kind projects, each piece is created with careful attention to detail.

Custom Woodcraft

Every piece of wood has its own character, and every project starts with an idea. Crafted Timber Studio combines creative design, precision cutting, layered construction, and hands-on finishing to create distinctive woodcraft made with attention to detail.

From decorative wall art and layered designs to functional pieces and unique creations, each project is an opportunity to turn wood into something worth displaying, giving, and keeping.

Personalized Gifts

The best gifts have a story behind them. Crafted Timber Studio can personalize a piece with a name, meaningful saying, special date, or design, or take the idea further and create something completely unique around the person receiving it.

That might mean turning a client’s motorcycle, favorite hobby, or meaningful image into custom wood art. The goal is to create a gift that feels personal because it was made specifically for one person and one story.

About the Studio

Crafted Timber Studio was created from a love of making things and seeing an idea become something real. Every project begins with a design, an inspiration, or sometimes simply a conversation about what someone would like to create.

I enjoy combining modern laser technology with hands-on craftsmanship to create pieces that are distinctive, personal, and made with care. Some projects begin with wood and a design. Others begin with a photograph, a favorite saying, a motorcycle, a special memory, or an idea that hasn’t been made yet.

For me, the most rewarding projects are the ones that become more than just an object. They become something worth displaying, giving, remembering, and keeping.
